Ambient Uploader

Ambient Uploader is a private, single-user automation tool built and used only by its owner.

It uploads the owner's own ambient-sound videos and Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel, CTRL SHIFT, using the YouTube Data API. It also sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlist membership for those uploads, and reads basic channel statistics for the owner's own reporting.

The app is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not provide a sign-up or login service for anyone else. Access is granted only by the owner, to the owner's own Google account, for the owner's own channel.
